A calm, focused dashboard for traders who actually want to learn from their trades.

1

In short

Most trading journals are either bloated SaaS products or a forgotten Excel sheet. ForexPulse sits between them — connected to your live MT5 account, but quiet enough that you'll actually open it.

It reads closed positions straight from IC Markets MT5, shows the four numbers that matter (total trades, total profit, best and worst trade), and lets you keep written reflections against each trade — plus weekly and monthly goals. The brief: open the app, see how the week went in under 10 seconds, write one paragraph, close the app.

2

Highlights

Integration

Live MT5 connection

Authenticates against IC Markets MT5 and syncs closed positions in the background — no imports, it's just there.

Product

Journal & goals, not noise

Per-trade reflections, daily/weekly summaries, and profit or win-rate targets the dashboard quietly tracks. No charts you'll never open.

Engineering

Node + Python backend

Node serves the REST API and auth; a small Python service talks to MT5, polls for new positions and reconciles them on the server.

Design

Calm by default

One accent colour, generous spacing, zero red until something actually goes wrong. The first version had charts and heatmaps — cutting them made the product better.
